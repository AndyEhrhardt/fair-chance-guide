# California hiring guidance

Source review on November 10, 2026. The detailed state guidance covers applicants for ordinary private-company jobs based in California. The five-employee threshold concerns the employer, so counting only coworkers at the job site can be misleading. State and federal coverage are evaluated separately. A reviewed source date records when the material was checked, not approval by counsel.

## How answers select guidance

| Situation and matching answers | Rule and limits | Practical next step | Official source |

| Private company, five or more employees, ordinary position | State Fair Chance Act path. Positions subject to legally required criminal screening or hiring restrictions and certain specified positions can be exceptions. A company policy alone does not establish one. | Confirm a stated exception against the law for the actual duties. | [CRD overview][scope], [applicant FAQ][faq] |
| Unknown size or 1–4 employees | Uncertain or below the statewide threshold. Neither answer establishes absence of all rights. | Check employer-wide size and local rules. Federal third-party report guidance still matches independently. | [CRD overview][scope], [FTC][ftc] |
| Other employer type, claimed or uncertain legal mandate, or potential regulated duties | Detailed state conclusions and response periods are withheld. Some public, staffing and other employers can be covered. Industry alone never establishes exemption. | Confirm who employs the applicant and which law, if any, governs the position. | [Applicant FAQ][faq] |
| Before an offer, or applicant explicitly reports being asked about convictions before an offer | Covered employers generally must delay conviction inquiries and consideration until a conditional offer. A later hiring stage alone does not establish when the question was asked. | Save the question, date and offer sequence. Contact CRD for help evaluating it. | [CRD overview][scope], [applicant FAQ][faq] |
| Conditional offer, or confirmed risk/withdrawal, with a potentially usable conviction | Individualized review considers the offense, elapsed time since conduct and sentence completion, and the job. Reported ranges provide context without a disqualification score or automatic expiry. | Compare the actual job duties with optional evidence of work, training or rehabilitation. | [Applicant FAQ][faq], [2 CCR § 11017.1(c)][reg] |
| Conviction may cost a conditional offer | Preliminary written notice must identify the conviction, include any conviction-history report and explain response rights and deadline. The employer must consider the response. | Ask for the notice and report if missing. Keep the notice and response. | [Applicant FAQ][faq], [approved regulation][reg] |
| Preliminary decision and written notice received | At least five business days from receipt. Additional time for accuracy disputes requires timely written notice to the employer that the applicant disputes accuracy and is taking specific steps to obtain evidence. Disputing only with the reporting company does not meet this condition. | Notify the employer of the dispute and evidence sought. Confirm receipt and the response deadline. | [2 CCR § 11017.1(c)(2)(E)–(F)][reg] |
| Final withdrawal of a conditional offer because of a conviction | Written final notice, any available reconsideration process, and right to complain to CRD. A rejection alone does not prove this sequence or reason. | Check the final notice, save the correspondence and consider CRD intake. | [Applicant FAQ][faq], [CRD complaint intake][complaint] |
| Arrest without conviction, or conviction marked sealed, dismissed or expunged | General limits on use. Pending cases and certain health-facility positions need separate analysis. A general “pardoned” or “certificate” answer does not identify the precise legal relief. | Verify the legal outcome and compare it with the reported entry. | [Applicant FAQ, question 4][faq] |
| Report from outside company, no final decision reported | In ordinary hiring, separate written disclosure and written permission generally precede procurement. Before adverse action based on the report, the employer must supply the report and federal rights summary. No universal federal five-day deadline is assigned. | Obtain the report and rights summary before a report-based rejection. | [FTC][ftc], [15 USC § 1681b(b)][fcra] |
| Outside report, possible error | Dispute inaccurate or incomplete information with the reporting company. This path does not depend on the state five-employee threshold or the employer withdrawing an offer. | Identify the entry, send supporting copies, notify the employer and request delivery of the corrected report. | [FTC][ftc], [15 USC § 1681i][dispute] |
| Outside report and final rejection or withdrawal | If the report contributed to the decision, the adverse-action notice identifies the company and explains dispute and free-copy rights. A free copy can be requested within 60 days of receiving that notice. | Save the notice and request the report directly from the company. | [FTC][ftc], [15 USC § 1681m(a)][final] |
| Internal or unknown source, or possible specialized federal procedure | An employer’s own search does not itself establish third-party report use. Certain safety-regulated transportation applications made entirely remotely and certain federal security determinations have special procedures. | Identify the source and procedure. A dispute over an outside report is still shown if applicable. | [FTC][ftc], [15 USC § 1681b(b)(2)–(4)][fcra] |
| Private employer in a 15+ size range | Title VII generally requires at least 15 employees for 20 calendar weeks in the current or prior year. Inconsistent treatment because of race or national origin can raise a discrimination issue. | Save the explanation and relevant correspondence if different treatment is a concern. | [EEOC criminal records][eeoc], [EEOC employer coverage][size] |

## Questions for a future review

- City and county ordinances, including Los Angeles and San Francisco. Locality text currently establishes no California municipal rule.
- California consumer reporting statutes, including the Investigative Consumer Reporting Agencies Act. Federal report rights are one layer of protection.
- Position-specific mandates and licensing, public or joint employment, and how size is counted for related entities. The current flags indicate a need for verification rather than an automatic occupational exemption.
- Pending and juvenile matters, diversion, particular pardons and relief certificates. The broad questionnaire cannot resolve exact legal status.
- How the notice was delivered and whether an employer allowed extra time. California has rules for deemed receipt. The app shows minimum periods and avoids individual due-date calculations.

The prompts use categories and yes/no answers. The app does not ask for, collect, or upload court papers, reports, notices, identifiers or dates. Suggested documents are for the applicant to keep or share independently.
